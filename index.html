About Embroshyal

Redefining modern spaces in the Chandigarh Tricity

In the evolving landscape of modern architecture and interior solutions, Embroshyal Private Limited has established itself as a trusted name in the Chandigarh Tricity for quality, innovation and craftsmanship. With over three decades of industry experience, we specialise in premium modular kitchens, wardrobes, aluminium system windows, uPVC doors & windows, toilet cubicles and acoustic panel solutions — tailored for contemporary residential and commercial spaces.

Driven by a commitment to precision, functionality and customer satisfaction, Embroshyal combines modern design aesthetics with durable engineering to create spaces that are both practical and visually refined.
