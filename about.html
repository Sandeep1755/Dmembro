Our Story

One partner from the first measurement to the final walkthrough

Emroshyal began with a simple conviction: a door should close as precisely as a kitchen should cook — and interiors should be built like furniture, not patched like fittings. Today we design, fabricate, install and service everything we sell, from our base in the Industrial Area of Panchkula.

Because fabrication, installation and after-care sit under one roof, there is no finger-pointing between vendors. Your site team talks to our factory. Your warranty is our warranty.

  • Factory-direct quality. Profiles, glass, hardware and lamination are cut, machined and assembled in our own unit.

  • One accountable team. The crew that starts your project finishes it — no subcontractor roulette.

  • Human handovers. Every client receives a walkthrough, a filled-in care card and a direct line to the director.
